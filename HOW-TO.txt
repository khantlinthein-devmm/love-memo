HOW TO MAKE THE WEBSITE YOURS  (about 10 minutes)
==================================================

1) PHOTOS
   - Put your photos inside the "photos" folder.
   - Rename them 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg ... 9.jpg
     (or keep your own names and change them in index.html)
   - More or fewer photos? Add or remove lines in the "photos:" list.

2) NAMES & WORDS
   - Open index.html in a text editor (Notepad, TextEdit, VS Code).
   - Search for:  const CONFIG
   - Change: yourName, herName, the story (timeline), reasons, and the letter.
   - Keep the "quotes" and the commas at the end of each line.

2b) THE SURPRISES (all inside CONFIG too)
   - secret:      the question she must answer first. Put every way she
                  might type the answer in "answers". Use  answers: []  to turn it off.
   - scratchPrize: what's hidden under the scratch card.
   - coupons:     love coupons she can tap to "redeem".
   - bucketList:  things to do together; she can tick them off.
   - timeCapsule: a letter that stays locked until "openOn".
   - question / noLines: the last question, where the "No" button runs away.
   Coupons and bucket-list ticks are saved in HER browser on HER phone.
   Note: anyone who opens the page's source code can read the answer
   and the time-capsule letter. It's a sweet lock, not a real safe.

3) SEE IT
   - Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.

4) OPTIONAL MUSIC
   - Put a song in the folder, e.g. song.mp3
   - In CONFIG set:  music: "song.mp3",

5) SEND IT TO HER (free link that works on her phone)
   - Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
   - Drag the WHOLE "anniversary-website" folder onto the page.
   - You get a link. Send it to her tomorrow ♥

TIPS
   - Double-click/tap anywhere on the page for a burst of hearts.
   - Photos from a phone can be big. That's OK, but under 2 MB each loads faster.
